A top-down heist game for the make.fun [best-heist-game-20261122-0559](https://make.fun/bounty/best-heist-game-20261122-0559) bounty.

Slip into the Velvet Vault, steal the Velvet Diamond from its pedestal, grab the gold bars on the way, and get out the far door before the alarm hits 100. Three guards patrol with sweeping vision cones, two cameras watch the hall, and the tall display cases are the only thing between you and being spotted. Throw coins to pull a guard away, pop smoke to blind the cameras, and sneak when a cone sweeps your way.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server (`node serve.ts`) and open `http://localhost:8080`. Move with WASD or arrows, hold Shift to sneak, Q or click to throw a coin, Space or F for smoke. Everything is plain files under `game/` — no network, no build step.
